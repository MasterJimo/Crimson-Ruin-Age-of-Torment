ABOUT CRIMSON RUIN

Age of Torment — survive the horde and master forbidden powers.

18+ · Mature content advisory: This game includes gothic horror, frightening enemies, dark themes, and stylised fantasy violence with blood-themed magic. It is intended for an adult audience. The 18+ label on the arcade catalogue is a creator-provided advisory, not an official ratings-board classification.

Crimson Ruin: Age of Torment is a single-player dark-fantasy arena survival roguelike created by MrJim. Fight monsters, gather experience, choose upgrades, survive escalating waves and confront bosses. No account or installation is required to play in a compatible browser.

How to play

Choose Easy or Hardcore mode, then select an element: Blood, Fire, Lightning or Poison. Move around the arena while your elemental spells automatically target enemies. Collect experience crystals to level up and select power-ups; special shrines, cursed reliquaries and Ascensions add further choices.

Desktop controls

  • WASD or arrow keys — move in any direction.
  • P, O or Esc — pause and open Character Stats / Encyclopedia; press again to resume.
  • The on-screen sound controls adjust Music, SFX and Mute.

Phone and tablet controls

Use the on-screen analog joystick to move in any direction. Release it to stop. Use the PAUSE button to open Character Stats and the Encyclopedia. The interface adapts to portrait and landscape orientations, in both Easy and Hardcore mode.

Progress and powers

You begin with health and grow stronger by collecting XP and choosing power-ups. Enemies become more dangerous over time; bosses and rare champions offer special rewards. Explore the in-game Encyclopedia for detailed mechanics, enemy information and optional Hunter's Oaths.

Saved information

Personal records, audio preferences, combat clarity settings and unlocked Hunter's Oaths may be saved in your browser. They are not automatically synced across devices. Read the Privacy policy for details.
