**Edits:** Original WAV/FLAC recordings converted into mono 24 kHz / 24 kbps MP3 attack cues, transient-focused cropping, peak normalization, 4ms fade-in and 25ms fade-out. No original recordings are claimed to be authored by this game.

The game limits regular attack sounds to 7 triggers per second, with compression and a maximum of two overlapping instances. This does not limit actual attacks or projectiles.
